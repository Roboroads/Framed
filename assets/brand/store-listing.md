## Full description

Framed is a local-area game you play with friends IRL: frame someone before you get framed.

Gather 3 or more people, pick a play area, and start. Everyone gets a secret target. To take yours out, photograph them in the app when they're not paying attention. The other players see your photo next to the target's reference selfie and vote: is this them? A majority yes and they're framed. You inherit their target, and the circle tightens until one player is left.

Your phone keeps the hunt moving. At a fixed interval, every player's compass fires at the same moment: an arrow and a distance to your target, gone again after a few seconds. If the people around you check their phones at exactly the same time, that's the tell.

The host draws the play area on a map and the server enforces it. Leave the circle too long and your framer sees your exact position. Stay gone longer and the game frames you.

Two ways to win: most confirmed frames when the game ends (default), or last player standing.

Privacy is part of the design. No account, no ads, no analytics or crash reporting. Your name, selfie, frame photos, and chat are encrypted on your phone before upload; the key travels only inside the join QR code and never reaches the server, so the server cannot read any of it. Location is the exception: the server reads your last known position to run the compass and the play area, and stores no movement history. Everything is deleted when the game ends, or after 24 hours at the latest.

You need a group of 3 or more, a camera, and location access (including background location while a game runs). The game is free and has no monetization.
